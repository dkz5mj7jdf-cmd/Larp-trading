About this demo

This is a simulation. Every price, move, trade and result is randomly generated inside the app. The coin tickers are real market coins, but nothing shown here is real data about them.

It doesn’t connect to any exchange or wallet, doesn’t trade real money, and isn’t financial advice. These results say nothing about how a real bot would perform.
